Decisions we need from you

4 rulings

Each of these is a place where the two documents give different answers. We are not able to pick for you — they change how the system behaves for your team every day.

D1 Does a direct-extension call create a ticket?

The highest-impact contradiction in the set. Build it the mock-up's way and every internal desk-to-desk call opens a support ticket.

Your workbook says"For Support line(s) = create ticket. If direct extension dial = Do not create ticket"
Your mock-ups say"Call Activity automatically creates a Support Ticket" — stated unconditionally on both pages, and the unanswered branch has no line-type condition either
We assumeThe workbook rule is correct — tickets only from support lines. Confirm and we proceed.

D2 What should an unanswered call produce?

Your workflow triggers no pop-up for unanswered calls, known caller or unknown, but still creates a ticket. With no pop-up there is no rep input, so the category can never be set — the instruction to "set ticket category, if known" cannot be satisfied on that branch. For unanswered and unknown together, the ticket has no contact, no company, no category and no owner.

In practice that means a blank ticket for every missed call, wrong number and robocall that reaches a support line.

Option ACreate only when a voicemail is left — the caller has actually asked for something
Option BCreate on every unanswered call, auto-closing untouched ones on a timer
We recommendOption A, and it is the cheaper build.

D3 Which moment is the activity date?

Your documents define this timestamp three ways: when the call was logged, the start of the call, and when the call was answered. Any time-to-answer or handling-time report depends on the choice. We suggest call start, so inbound and outbound measure the same way, with answer time derived separately if you need it.

Related: the sheet's note on Create date says HubSpot defines it as "the date and time the call took place". It does not — it is when the record was created, and it is read-only. Reports grouped on it will be wrong for any migrated call.

D4 Do outbound calls pop the form?

Your mapping describes outbound behaviour for the number fields and your assignment rule covers the employee "accepting or placing" the call — but the only diagram is titled Incoming Support Call Workflow and branches solely on inbound. Whether an outbound call raises the pop-up, and whether it can open a ticket, is undefined.

Where that leaves the mapping sheet

Seven of the properties the sheet asks the integration to write are read-only in your portal, which is why several items above resolve as "already handled" rather than as work: voicemail available, record source, from- and to-number names, inbound answer status, activity created by, and the transcript and summary fields. The likeliest explanation is that the mapping was drawn from HubSpot's full property catalogue without filtering on the read-only column — so the fix is mechanical, not a design argument.

Three smaller items to clean while the sheet is open: hs_call_has_transcript, hs_createdate and hs_created_by_user_id each appear twice with different notes; enterprise_number_unique_2 carries a _2 suffix that usually means an earlier property claimed the name, so it is worth confirming which one is live; and Dynamics Regarding is mapped only to Company, though it can point at a case or another entity, and those references would be dropped on migration.

Transcript and call summary are annotated in your sheet as future use but sit in the phase-one mapping. We have treated them as phase two.
